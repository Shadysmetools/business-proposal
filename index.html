Questions

Why is this cheaper than an Odoo agency?

Agencies pay for sales teams, offices and account managers. You work directly with the consultant who builds your system. The scope is fixed and written down before work starts.

What if we outgrow it?

Odoo runs companies with thousands of users. As you grow you add modules such as HR, manufacturing or an online store. Nothing is rebuilt, and your data stays where it is. Moving from Starter to Business costs only the difference.

Who owns the data and the system?

You do. The server, domain and database are registered in your company's name, and you get the admin passwords. You are never locked in.

What happens after the support period?

You keep everything. Ongoing support is optional at USD 100 a month, cancel any time. Daily backups keep running either way.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. The system and website work in Arabic and English, and the AI assistant answers in Egyptian Arabic, English and Franco.
