Questions

Why is this cheaper than an Odoo agency?

Agencies pay for sales teams, offices and account managers. You work directly with the consultant who builds your system. The scope is fixed and written down before work starts.

What if we outgrow it?

Odoo runs companies with thousands of users. As you grow you add modules such as HR, manufacturing or an online store. Nothing is rebuilt. Moving from Starter to Business costs only the difference.

Who owns the data and the system?

You do. The server, domain, database and every subscription are in your company's name, and you get the admin passwords. You are never locked in.

What happens after the support period?

You keep everything. Ongoing support is optional at USD 100 a month, cancel any time. Daily backups keep running either way.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. The system and website work in Arabic and English, and the AI assistant answers in Egyptian Arabic, English and Franco.

  • Up to 15 users, 1 company, 1 warehouse, 1 currency (EGP)
  • Odoo Community with standard workflows. Egyptian e-invoicing (ETA), payroll and full accounting are quoted separately
  • Data import: customers, products and opening stock, up to 5,000 rows, from our Excel template, imported once
  • Website: up to the package's page count, you provide text and images, 2 rounds of revisions
  • 3 printed layouts (quotation, invoice, delivery note) and up to 5 dashboard tiles
  • AI assistant answers from your approved content (up to 20 pages) and hands complex questions to your staff
  • WhatsApp needs Meta business verification and a dedicated number; Meta's approval is outside my control
  • All subscriptions and accounts are in your name and paid by you; I only get admin access to set them up

  • Anything beyond these limits (new modules, integrations, extra warehouses or companies) is quoted as an add-on before work starts
